About Me

The person behind the campaigns

I am Aditi Sharma, a digital marketing specialist who works with founders, small teams, and marketing leads who want their online presence to actually bring in business, not just look good.

My Background

I started out managing social media pages for a handful of local businesses, mostly because I was curious about why some posts got attention and others disappeared. That curiosity turned into a full time career once I saw how much a clear marketing plan could change a small business, not through luck, but through consistent, measured work.

Over the years I have worked across industries including retail, healthcare, SaaS, hospitality, and professional services. Each industry taught me something different about how people search, compare, and decide, and I bring all of that experience into every new project.

Today I work as an independent digital marketing specialist, partnering closely with a small number of clients at a time so that every account gets proper attention instead of being one of fifty on a spreadsheet.

How I Work

Every project starts with listening. I ask about your business goals, your current customers, and what has or has not worked before. From there I put together a plan that is specific to your situation rather than a generic template applied to every client.

I keep communication simple and regular, usually through short weekly or bi weekly updates, so you always know what stage a project is at and what results to expect.

Values I Work By

Honesty over hype

I will tell you when a channel is not worth your budget, even if that means recommending less work for me.

Ownership

Every campaign I run, I treat as if it were my own business on the line.

Continuous learning

Marketing platforms change constantly, so I spend time every week reading, testing, and staying current.

Respect for budgets

Whether a client has a large budget or a modest one, I aim to spend it as if it were my own money.
