About Me

Aditi Sharma Digital Marketing Specialist

Aditi Sharma is a digital marketing specialist and digital marketing expert who works with founders, small teams, and marketing leads who want their online presence to actually bring in business, not just look good on paper.

My Background

Aditi Sharma started out managing social media pages for a handful of local businesses, mostly out of curiosity about why some posts got attention while others disappeared. That curiosity turned into a full time career once it became clear how much a clear marketing plan could change a small business, not through luck, but through consistent, measured work.

Over the years, Aditi Sharma has worked across industries including retail, healthcare, SaaS, hospitality, and professional services. Each industry brought a different lesson about how people search, compare, and decide, and all of that experience now feeds into every new project as a digital marketing specialist.

Today, Aditi Sharma works as an independent digital marketing expert, partnering closely with a small number of clients at a time so that every account gets proper attention instead of being one of fifty on a spreadsheet.

How I Work

Every project with Aditi Sharma starts with listening. She asks about your business goals, your current customers, and what has or has not worked before. From there, she puts together a plan that is specific to your situation rather than a generic template applied to every client.

As a digital marketing expert, Aditi Sharma keeps communication simple and regular, usually through short weekly or bi weekly updates, so you always know what stage a project is at and what results to expect next.
